Our Story

How it all began

"We can really say we've crossed an ocean for each other."

Our story goes back to a hot summer day in June 2019, filled with the smell of chlorine in the air — poolside at an international LGBT swim competition in Flushing, Queens. Daniel approached Nick with a smooth opener, asking if he could take a photo with "New York" (the team logo across Nick's swimsuit, from his team the New York Front Runners). That quickly turned into drinks and dinner, followed by a transatlantic long-distance romance that lasted a short nine months… before a global pandemic started shutting international borders.

With news of the US border closing, Daniel had to make a rapid-fire decision. Could he make it to the US before the borders were shut for good? Heading straight for the airport, he made it on one of the last flights out of France — and we were able to spend the start of the pandemic together in a tiny apartment in Brooklyn.

Going from long-distance to "New York on pause" together was shockingly easy. We settled into an easy rhythm: runs around Prospect Park, socially distanced picnics with friends, bagel Fridays from our local shop, and finally just being in the same city as one another.

But as 2020 wore on, it became clear Daniel wouldn't be able to renew his visa. With shifting rules and the French border still technically closed, Nick started looking for a way into France. The only option was a student visa — so Nick applied to one masters program, which happened to be entirely in French, a language he barely spoke at the time. With some gay magic clearly on our side, the university accepted him, and he moved to France in August 2020.

Since then, we've been living in Paris, going through six years of adventures together: masters programs, new jobs, bike trips, hikes, travels across Europe, the bureaucratic nightmares of being an international couple — and buying and renovating our own apartment in Paris.

We are so excited to be celebrating our wedding day with our international family, and we are so grateful to all of you for filling our lives with so much love.
